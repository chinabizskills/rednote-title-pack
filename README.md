## What it does

On RedNote a note lives or dies in the first 10 characters: the title decides the click, the cover text decides the dwell. Most creators guess.

Decomposes high-CTR titles into a reusable 12-formula library plus usage rules, keyword placement, and clean A/B variants. RedNote's international wave makes this a cross-border content cheat code.

## What you get

- 12 proven viral title formulas with examples (number promise, identity insertion, counter-intuition, pitfall warning, result-first, contrast conflict, help-me, listicle, suspense gap, time anchor, location lock, stacked benefit).
- Usage rules: title <= 16 char-equivalent, hook inside first 5; first body line restates the promise; cover text <= 6 words.
- Keyword placement: 1 search term in title, natural 2-3x in body, 3-5 hashtags.
- Every output: 5 titles + 2 cover-copy sets, each labeled with its formula for clean A/B testing.
