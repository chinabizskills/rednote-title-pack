# RedNote Title & Cover Copy Pack

## What problem it solves

On RedNote a note lives or dies in the **first 10 characters**: the title decides the click, the cover text decides the dwell. This pack decomposes high-CTR titles into a reusable formula library and outputs ready-to-post titles + cover text.

## 12 viral title formulas (with examples)

1. **Number promise**: "3 moves, dark circles visibly faded"
2. **Identity insertion**: "Office workers must-read | Lunchbox for a week, eating well on $7/day"
3. **Counter-intuition**: "Stop washing your hair daily! A scalp doctor explains…"
4. **Pitfall warning**: "The 5 places you should never cheap out on a reno — I hit them all"
5. **Result-first**: "Used it 30 days — pores actually smaller to the naked eye"
6. **Contrast conflict**: "Baby room at $500 vs $5,000 — what actually differs"
7. **Help-me** (highest engagement): "Girls, help — did I get scammed on this?"
8. **Listicle**: "It's 2026 and you still don't know these 8 sites"
9. **Suspense gap**: "The one thing nobody told me before buying…"
10. **Time anchor**: "Started writing at 30 — too late?"
11. **Location/scenario lock**: "Hangzhou with kids | Empty, free, and photogenic"
12. **Stacked benefit**: "No dieting, no carb-counting, waist -4cm in a month"

## Usage rules

- Title ≤ 16 characters-equivalent; hook or audience word inside the first 5.
- First body line restates the title promise; cover text ≤ 6 words, large font, not a verbatim repeat of the title.
- Keyword placement: 1 search term in the title, appearing naturally 2–3× in the body, 3–5 hashtags.
- Every output: 5 titles + 2 cover-copy sets, each labeled with its formula for clean A/B.

## Boundaries

No virality-rate guarantees; no fabricated numbers or medical claims; "my real experience" framing only when the user actually has that experience.
